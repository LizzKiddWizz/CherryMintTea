Personality

Kana on first impression is aloof and appears to lack all interest in his surroundings and in the people occupying it. He is distrustful due to past experiences and does not like being cornered by those he does not know of or has never met. With a tendency to judge first and reflect later, he can come off as prickly and avoidant. At which it is best to retreat and attempt at a later date. Pushing him further despite his obvious discomfort has adverse effects, making him out to be cold in demeanor and cruel with his words when he truthfully is everything but. Kana knows that his reaction is improper and does try to mitigate it, but it comes from a place of anxiety and terror. He always apologizes after. Though, to have him trust you is not difficult. It merely takes time and patience by continually exposing him to you in short bursts. Then and only then can one witness the breadth of his personality. Kana is a motherly individual, often doting on those he cares for. Which is many, as he knows a large amount of people. He's loyal, due in part to his ability to perpetually perceive the past and future, as well as his willingness to dedicate his all to those who were willing to see beyond his nastiness. Physically affectionate, he prefers to have at least one hand on a person at all times. He is rather generous, giving gifts frequently and setting aside time for others when they need him. His most consistent mood is happiness, and he is rarely seen without a smile. This aides him in spreading said happiness to those around him. Kana also does not like to lie, and isn't very good at doing so, as such he is always expressing exactly what he feels in the moment. Likely, this means more often than not, he is truly jovial. But others have noticed that it is often mixed with other emotions only perceptible through his eyes. The nosy type, he gets involved in most anything with the intention to assist others. He cares not about the time wasted or whether he gets anything out of it (or if the person wanted him to intervene in the first place), but it means all the same to him. Kana recalls everything anyone tells him, as his memory is astounding. This also applies to anything he has seen, heard, or read, which has caused others to falsely believe he is a genius. He is smart, but not to the extent most think he is, as he struggles to apply the knowledge he has in a practical environment without immense amounts of practice. Kana is well loved by those who know him, and is often surrounded by others and rarely ever by himself. He appreciates that as he dislikes being alone with his thoughts. But it has the consequence of giving him a good reputation that tends to precede him. Which then causes people he may not know to interact with him amicably without properly introducing themselves.

History

Past: Kana was born to two young parents, unequipped to raise their unanticipated set of twins. His mother opted to give him up to her uncle, afraid of what may happen when her and her boyfriend's parents found out about their children. Since her uncle owned an orphanage, they figured it'd be the best cover up, and a good place for their kids to grow up. Kana and his brother Amodeus were inseparable, and went everywhere together. They enjoyed helping their uncle about the orphanage the best they could, and playing every once in a while with the other children. But when Kana was still young, he contracted Braekas, similar to human Gargoyles disease, where the face is petrified in one singular expression. Sarsenee observe the face to verify intention, words are only meant to solidify the intent. Body language isn't considered at all. And thus Kana was an outcast, his features not quite conveying what he spoke. A forever neutral face, doomed to be distrusted by his peers. Much of his adolescence was spent in obscurity. He helped his uncle with the orphanage often enough, but usually hid himself inside his room otherwise. School wasn't an option for him, especially since the disease he was afflicted with wasn't entirely understood. His uncle chose to homeschool him instead. Amodeus was a shy boy, and being left without his twin at school allowed him to come out of his shell more. Yet it also bore cruelty in his heart as well. His sweet demeanor fell away along with his preference of his brother's company over others. When the two reached adulthood, Amodeus went from indifference to vindictiveness. Harshly ripping into Kana verbally whenever their uncle was not around. His friends had pressured him to quit being around his brother, corrupting their relationship to the point of no return. In the earlier years of their adulthood, Amodeus went as far as to disown Kana, and the two never spoke to one another again. Kana went on to graduate from university with full honors and became employed at a robotics firm, leaving the orphanage behind. This meant he was often forced to be out in the open and interact with a lot more people than he ever had. With this, he met Cyren Sinclair. He had been the only person who was friendly to him in a long time. Being from a neighboring planet, and from a different species, he was not as off-putt by Kana like the others. He had even told Kana he wasn't as expressionless as people claimed. They got along well, Cyren often flirting with him and being affectionate. Kana wasn't used to such attention and didn't realize what Cyren may have wanted from him until the man bluntly asked him out. The two were smitten with one another, and were together for nearly a decade. Kana was happy that someone had seen him and wanted him after being ignored for ages. The reason they did not marry sooner was that there was still contention from Cyren's parents. They were worried about Kana's condition and the possibility he might pass it to his children, if he even could conceive. Eventually Cyren proposed without their blessing about 6 months before Kana's birthday. They were to be wed then, and finally together always. It did not come to fruition, however. Kana was murdered by his employer. Stabbed through the neck, a portion being drilled through, up into his brain. When he next awoke, he was an athalmus, and no longer impacted by Braekas. For a short period of time he wandered about the Nilladexum of NewAge and gathered his bearings. And then for eons after, he was an enraged being, the embodiment of death. He reaped souls with the savagery of a warmonger, slicing them separate from their bodies in indifferent cruelty. No one knows quite why there was such a drastic change. Eventually he mellowed and set out to earn his other keys. Delliore took him under his tutelage to hopefully catch him up with the athalmus that were designated around the same time he was. On his last key, Kana met Godellious. His attitude was still indifferent, but that did not stop the young athalmus from following him around everywhere. He only played nice as that is what Delliore commanded. He taught Godellious how to read, and in several human languages. As that is what Kana was working through at the time, human literature. Both of them were enthralled by the many stories and genres there were, considering their backgrounds. So they were often seen reading together. Since Godellious was a recluse, once Kana was promoted, they were assigned the same group, with Kana as it's leader. Soon after, Mercusious became their third. Unknowingly, this would set off a cascade of events that would force Kana to learn the heavy consequences that his words and actions carried.

Present: He has as of recently been trying to contact Godellious, worried about the state of Ascension. There hasn't been much of a response and that is concerning as some of the higher choir angels have become sick. On top of that, he found out his universe is scheduled for demolition soon, and has been desperately trying to persuade another version of his brother to help him petition against it. He is unwilling to ask the other versions of himself despite the encroaching deadline. The amount of stress he is under has been getting to him, to the point he has been forgetting important dates (this is unusual for those with domain over time). Which is unfortunate timing as he had been alerted that a new athalmus would be joining their group soon. Kana wants to prepare himself to meet him without much conflict, but has been too busy to look at his file to gauge what type of person he is. All he knows is his general appearance and credentials. It bothers him that his physical age is young, as the age they appear as is often when they passed on if they haven't matured beyond it yet.

Future: One of Kana's dreams is to have children of his own. Not create life for a certain purpose, like the denizens of Ataraxia, but children from his body, made to find their own reason for living. He's had this dream since he was alive, and it had carried into how he interacts with others, especially since he has never been able to achieve it. And since he has been focused on meeting others to their happy endings, whether they consent or not, he has been furthering the distance between himself and his own dreams.

Mercusious Thiabult
Best Friend

Mercusious is someone that Kana would do anything for. He has proven his loyalty and then some to the sarsen, enough that he always says yes to his requests. He finds Merc's nonchalance and loyalty endearing. The dedication to his work (even if it appears he is simply dragging his feet through it) and to his people is a quality he often compliments the man on. He is also one of the few people that acknowledged Merc's intelligence, much to his chagrin. Most would say Kana is Merc’s babysitter with the amount of crude comments he makes and his tendency to 'forget' his work; leading to Kana having to reprimand him even after hours. But that is the furthest thing from the truth. Merc teases and jokes with Kana AFTER the sarsen has already provoked him. Proving the two egg one another on, even if it is not as noticeable on Kana's end. They have quite a bit of chemistry, and are probably dating in another life where Merc doesn't have a preference for women. Despite their camaraderie, Merc never seems to comfort Kana when he is under duress and it is a notable contradiction in their relationship.

Diablossa Lizeh
Close Friend

Diablossa is strange, in that she is a mortal that has inserted herself into his athalmial duties as if she has always belonged. Most mortals have a reverence for him that he dislikes but understands, as he is technically a deity in comparison to them. But after he had personally helped her through her struggles to purify her soul, she has become rather taken to him. She helps around his home, gets him to bed to make sure he sleeps, organizes his work, takes care of Absinthe, etc. Each duty she undertook slowly, one at a time, throughout the period they have known one another. The one of importance that he is grateful for is that she comforts him when he can no longer stand to leave his room. A task that used to be Mercusious' long ago has been claimed by her. She pulls him forward, just like he did for her. He does his best to repay her benevolence by cooking for her (she is terrified of fire and heat related things and thus cannot do it herself). They spend time drinking tea and chatting about the things they do when not together. He tailors her clothes, brushes her hair, and has realized that she is one of the things that make his duties as an athalmus worth it. He hates thinking about how someday she will have to leave him.

Jebidiah Leonard
Good Friend

Kana doesn't mind Jebidiah's quiet nature, they are often seen sitting together despite not interacting. When they do talk, Kana likes making Jeb laugh, and is very successful at it. He hopes Jeb can open up more to others and show his true colors, as they are quite bright and cheerful. So Kana makes sure to cook something for him if he knows they are meeting that day. He values his humor, compassion, and enjoys encouraging his hobbies by gifting him supplies. It isn't known by anyone whether Jeb is bothered by this or Kana's company, but it is assumed by how he never attempts to avoid him that they have a positive relationship.

Alamon Crescens
Good Friend

Kana enjoys having philosophical conversations with Alamon, even though he doesn't usually add anything to it. Merely asking questions and nodding every so often. He thinks Alamon should allow himself to open up and relax. Flex his intelligence considering he hides it behind ignorant indifference. That does not stop him from humoring his moronic pranks and escapades, though. He believes they are both parts of Alamon and deserve a chance to shine. Kana thinks Alamon is oddly pessimistic despite appearing optimistic, and that the contrast is funny given his frustrated expressions when people get the opposite impression. Sometimes they cook together and it reminds them both of their family members and their pasts. Leaving them oddly sentimental and yet filled with warmth.
