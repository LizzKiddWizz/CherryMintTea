Ataraxia

One of the many Arcana of Wednas. It is notable in how it manages it's souls. They are allowed to travel between the Majors. And are not barred from residing in the same one for the entirety of their afterlife. Ataraxia flows upwards, beginning from Lacrimae and ending at Ascension. One of the few arcana in Wednas to lack a suite (shield), it is susceptible to frequent attacks from the Great Beyond. This is due to it's size, the fact it does not forcefully purge souls of corruption upon admission, and that it retains and intakes a large amount of mortals. In order to maintain order and protect the majors, each set of denizens are charged with defending them from the threat of the Eterphage.
