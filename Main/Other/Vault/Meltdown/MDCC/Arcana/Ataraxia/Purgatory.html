Purgatory

Ataraxia's neutrally aligned major. Souls teetering on the edge of damnation and radiance come here to soothe the conflicting in their souls. It also serves as a transitional space for those ascending from Lacrimae. Purgatory is a place of knowledge and relaxation. Of learning about oneself, and of deciding who you would like to be going forward. Sometimes, mortals decide they would like to be someone entirely new, and thus reincarnate, leaving Atraxia with a cleaned soul for another chance at life. Purgatory itself is small, and contains mostly plants as it's native fauna. Although there are few nature locales, as the major is streamlined to it's purpose. This makes it the only major in Ataraxia that contains exclusively denizen-made structures. The denizens that reside within Purgatory are Circleans. They have no known paralells in most mortal cultures, as they appear different from common depictions of grim reapers. Circleans used to almost exclusively reap souls, but now focus on taking care of Purgatory's mortal residents, maintaining the beauty of Purgatory, and training new Death Brigade members. They are powerful denizens, considered the strongest when compared to Ascensia and Lacra. Due to this, they are Ataraxia's last line of defense, and are dispatched when the other two majors are incapable of handling a threat alone. Any escapades they undertake outside of Ataraxia are purely personal. They are trained to limit interaction with the living when under reaping directives.
