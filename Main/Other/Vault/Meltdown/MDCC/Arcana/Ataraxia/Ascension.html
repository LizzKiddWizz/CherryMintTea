Ascension

Ataraxia's light aligned major, it takes in souls with little sin and provides them a paradise sculpted to their needs and desires. Meant as a realm of rewards for maintaining good morality, it contains the most environmental variability in all of Ataraxia. However, much of the fauna is preexisting, often introduced at the whims of the living. Much of the space is made with the intention of evoking nostalgia, or to live out a dream or wish. Contrary to what you may think, it is uniformly structured, areas organizied according to their theming with districting used to prevent lapses in immersion. With over fifty cities, Ascension's population to land ratio is heavily skewed. It can house the entirety of Lacrimae and Purgatory's mortal residents and still have room for more. This is driven by the constant need to expand to provide more variety for the dead. Those that take on this work are it's denizens, Ascensia. They are most commonly known as angels by their mortal dependents due to their wings, halos, and association with the light. They maintain and protect Ascension with a militaristic efficiency without much input by the realm's creator. Which is an aspect not shared by it's neighboring majors. Ascensia have jobs related to promoting happiness, light, hope, and dreams. To spread knowledge, teach lessons in morality, and guide the living as they meander through life. Despite this, and unlike the other arcana in Ataraxia, they interact very little with living mortals. They send divinations, visions, and signs. Not showing themselves in person unless they absolutely must.
