Lacrimae

Ataraxia's dark aligned major, it houses the souls of the damned, beings filled with corruption. Lacrimae is different from most arcana of it's type. Souls are still punished, but not forcefully purified once the soul's consciousness is lost. Instead, mortals are encouraged to make ammends with themselves. They purify their souls through redemption, for a chance to ascend upwards to Ascension or Purgatory. Much of Lacrimae is shaped by it's denizens, whether sentient or not. But it is most similar to deserts (arctic and arid), wetlands, jungles, boreal, and taiga. The odd vairty stems from the cold temperatures in some levels, and the humidity in others. Yielding a wide variety of fauna that evolve sporadically and often, reflecting the disjointed nature of Lacrimae as well as it's creator. The major's denizens are known as Lacra, infernal beings that mortals mistake for demons and devils. They live within, maintain, and protect Lacrimae in Mercusious' stead. Lacra have jobs related to spreading chaos amongst the living, assisting the damned with redemption, and keeping the peace or balance of Lacrimae or mortal realms. They interact the most with the living in comparison to the other denizens of Ataraxia.
